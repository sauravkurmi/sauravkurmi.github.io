Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Channel Factory ("the app") is a private desktop tool. Its owner uses it to publish videos to, and read statistics from, YouTube channels that the owner manages. This policy explains what Google user data the app uses and how.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for these permissions, and only these:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload videos the owner made.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to confirm the right channel is signed in, and to list that channel's own videos and their public counts (views, likes, comments).
  • Manage the owner's videos (youtube.force-ssl): to set or correct the title, description and tags of videos the app uploaded.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read the channel's own statistics, such as views, watch time, average percentage viewed and subscribers gained.

The app does not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or any data of other YouTube users. It does not read viewers' personal information.

How the data is used

  • To upload and schedule the owner's videos and set their text.
  • To show the owner how their own channels' videos are performing.

The data is used for nothing else. No advertising, profiling or resale.

Storage and security

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in the user's application data folder, outside any project or shared folder. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • The app has no server and no database. Channel statistics are fetched when the owner asks for them, shown on screen, and not stored. The only exception is a local upload log (video ID, title, schedule time), which stays on the owner's computer.
  • All communication with Google uses HTTPS.

Sharing

The app does not share, sell, rent or transfer Google user data to anyone.

Limited Use

Channel Factory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party access or via the Google security settings page.
  • Deleting the app's local folder removes the stored tokens and log. Revoking access makes any stored token useless.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
